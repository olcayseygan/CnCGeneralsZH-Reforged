# Two log lines, both -scenario only. SCENARIO SHOT is every shot an aircraft fires: its dist is the
# weapon's own range measure (between the bounding spheres) and range the weapon's reach, so the first
# shot of a pass reads its distance against the reach. SCENARIO JET RUN is every frame a jet aims at a
# ground target: dist centre to centre in 2D, reach (range plus both spheres), breakoff, the turn
# radius, and BREAK OFF on the frame it turns away. The closest approach of a pass is the smallest dist
# between its BREAK OFF and the next run in; before the fix it fell to tens of units, the jet over the
# target. Done when every jet's closest approach stays near its breakoff's clearance (the larger of the
# minimum range and the turn radius) and the tallies still show the targets hit.
#
