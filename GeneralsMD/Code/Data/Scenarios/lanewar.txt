# Lane War director proof: four players on the lane map, two a team, buying units onto their plot
# grids with laneplace (LaneWarDirector::placeUnit, the entry point MSG_LANEWAR_ORDER reaches). The
# director does the rest: a spawn tick every 30 wall seconds (900 frames at 30 Hz), each team
# sending one seat's copy in turn (A1 and B1, then A2 and B2), income on every tick with the middle
# bonus for the team holding past the centre line, the copies dying 130 wall seconds after they
# spawn, and the match ending when a fortress falls. Team A buys more as its money comes in and B
# does not, so A takes the middle and in the end B's fortress. Read the LANEWAR PLACE,
# HEADLESS LANEWAR, LANEMARCH and HEADLESS LANEMARCH lines.
#
#   generals.exe -headless -quickstart -noshellmap -observer -multiInstance -noFPSLimit
#     -randommap 3 lane -autoskirmish 4 -takeover -side 0 FactionAmerica -side 1 FactionChina

#     -logPrefix lanewar_

# wave. Slot 0 is team A's seat 1 (start 1), slot 1 B's seat 1, slot 2 A's seat 2, slot 3 B's seat
